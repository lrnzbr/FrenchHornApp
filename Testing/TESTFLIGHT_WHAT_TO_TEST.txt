This beta includes the redesigned practice tools, musical/audio reliability fixes,
and two new practice improvements. Please prioritize:

Tuner
- Play a steady note, then stop. The final note, staff, cents and needle should
  remain for about 1.5 seconds before clearing.
- Play a different note during the hold: it should replace the previous result
  immediately. Pause should clear the result immediately.
- Check F/B-flat notation, flat/sharp response, background recovery, Siri, and
  available wired/Bluetooth routes. Paused listening should remain paused.

Metronome
- Tap the tempo number to enter a whole-number tempo from 40 to 208.
- Apply changes during playback; the pulse should continue without restarting.
- Check invalid values and Cancel, slider/plus/minus, Tap tempo, meter and accent.
- Relaunch after setting 96, 3/4, accent off: choices should return, with playback
  stopped. In 6/8 each click is an eighth note.

Also check fingering alternates, the reference tone's octave, and compact/large-text
layouts. Include version/build, device/OS, horn, audio route, steps and recovery in
any bug report. The full acceptance suite and run template are in Testing/.
